## 🎨 The Design

Your platform features a sophisticated design that reflects the luxury of your products:

- **Colors**: Deep navy (#28 depth) and warm gold accents
- **Typography**: Playfair Display for elegance + Montserrat for clarity
- **Textures**: Subtle diagonal crosshatch and film grain overlays
- **Animations**: Smooth, measured transitions (Framer Motion)
- **Mobile**: Fully responsive, mobile-first design

Every pixel has been considered. Every interaction is polished. Every detail serves the brand.
